# Research Digest: 2026-11-02

## Executive Pulse
* Ultra-long context efficiency saw significant architectural progress via hierarchical latent and tree-structured sparse attention mechanisms (such as LSA and ZHA), achieving order-of-magnitude speedups at million-token scales while preserving task accuracy.
* Training dynamics and post-training precision bottlenecks received critical scrutiny, with findings showing that standard round-to-nearest bf16 casting silently discards the vast majority of RL policy updates, which can be mitigated via unbiased rounding fixes like Fair Cast.
* Autonomous agentic systems demonstrated impressive capability leaps in complex, real-world stochastic environments—exemplified by AutoTavern building a competitive Hearthstone Battlegrounds AI and game simulator from scratch that outperforms top human players.

## Research & Papers
* Latent Sparse Attention: Towards Subquadratic Ultra-Long Context Attention | On GLM-5.3-FLASH (QWEN-3.8-27B) at 10M tokens, D-LSA speeds up one attention layer by 22.7x (72.4x) in prefill and 4.6x (42.5x) in decode over its original sparse attention (full attention) while keeping similar accuracy; N-LSA matches full attention accuracy with a 1.6x per-layer training speedup at 128k tokens | not stated | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42147
* Hierarchical World Models with Implicit Dynamics | Achieves a 38.5% improvement over state-of-the-art policy and diffusion-based planners, and a 135% performance gain over single-level world model MPC on complex, unseen long-horizon tasks | not stated | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42146
* Rethinking Time Series Tokenization from a Frequency Perspective | TimeFT consistently and significantly improves performance across forecasting, classification, and anomaly detection tasks | The method is parameter-free and incurs negligible computational complexity | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28997
* Build, Don't Play: Self-Improving LLM Research Agents Develop a Hearthstone Battlegrounds AI That Rivals Top Players | TavernAgent's rating rose from 6,000 to ~15,000; its last 20 records average a placement of 1.60 with 80% first places (better than the server's ten highest-rated players); answers 91% of play tasks in TavernBench in 0.03 s | Requires human intervention to set goals and rules on ambiguous mechanics | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33845
* Gradient Descent on Two ReLU Neurons: Global Landscape and Bifurcation Dynamics | Characterized loss landscape showing every critical point with both student weights nonzero is either a global minimum or saddle; student neurons first collapse toward a bisector saddle before escaping and specializing | Restricted to a well-specified two-neuron ReLU teacher-student model under isotropic Gaussian inputs | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42118
* bf16 Rounding Silently Discards Most RL Policy Updates: A Hidden Training-Inference Mismatch and an Unbiased One-Line Fix | Fair Cast outperforms round-to-nearest across 25 matched seed-pairs (ahead on 18, tied on 6, behind on 1 with a mean gain of 6.1 points); on zebra puzzles it reaches 0.57 +/- 0.16 vs 0.33 +/- 0.10 | Adds 22-38 ms per export of 1.7B parameters | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41073
* Subquadratic Attention with a Hierarchical Tree-Structured Indexer | ZHA constructs a tree of recursively compressed key summaries, resulting in asymptotically subquadratic scaling that dramatically outperforms competing methods beyond 1 million context | Requires a short KL distillation training stage over a few billion tokens | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42112
* There Is No Free Loop: When Does Looping a Trained Model Pay for Its Inference Compute? | Only the looped 0.8B model beats both equal-parameter and equal-FLOPs unlooped baselines across Qwen3 and Qwen3.5 models (0.8B to 9B) | At 4B and 9B scales, looped models do not beat the baseline trained without a loop | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42030
* Prune Gradually, Adapt Sparsely: Efficient Pruning of Large Language Models at Extreme Sparsity | ETAPS achieves lower perplexity than ELSA at every evaluated sparsity from 50% to 90% (reductions up to 42%), pruning LLaMA2-7B at 90% sparsity with 5.0x smaller persistent state and 49% fewer GPU-hours | Requires progressive stages of gradual weight removal and sparse adaptation | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41968
* Cost-Aware Stopping for Freeze-Thaw Bayesian Optimization: When Does One-Step Lookahead Suffice? | EI stopping is noninferior to correction in most high-cost settings while trading lower scores for fewer training increments on tested surrogates | Performance utility depends on the underlying surrogate and information sharing | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42085
* HIDRA: Hierarchical Dual-Routing Attention for Replay-Free Lifelong Imitation Learning | Consistently outperforms both replay-free and replay-based baselines, improving AUC and forward transfer while maintaining low forgetting on LIBERO benchmarks | Sensitive to high interference and language variation environments, though mitigated by dual-attention and geometric regularization | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36374
* HobbyLM: A Sparse Mixture-of-Experts Model Family on a Hobby Budget | HobbyLM-1B reaches a 47.5 average on seven zero-shot benchmarks; HobbyLM-Image generates 1024x1024 images in a 32x-compressed latent space | Trained from scratch with a few hundred to a few thousand dollars of rented GPU time | https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34582
